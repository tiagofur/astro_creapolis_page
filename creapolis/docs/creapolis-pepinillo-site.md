## 2. Quick Start Guide (creapolis.dev/pepinillo/guide)
### Welcome to Pepinillo
Pepinillo is designed to be simple yet powerful. Here is how to get started:

1. **Pick a Mode:** Swipe between Pomodoro, Flowtime, Time Block, or Stopwatch.
2. **Link a Task:** Tap "Link Task" to select what you're working on.
3. **Start Focusing:** Hit the big Start button.
4. **Mindful Breaks:** When the timer ends, follow the suggested break activities to recharge.

### Pro Tip: Live Activities
You don't need to keep the app open. Your timer will appear on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island automatically.

## 3. Focus Tips & Techniques (creapolis.dev/pepinillo/tips)
### Mastering your Focus
- **The 5-Minute Rule:** If a task feels daunting, set a 5-minute timer. Usually, once you start, you'll want to keep going.
- **Batching:** Use Time Blocks to group similar small tasks together.
- **The Flowtime Advantage:** If you find Pomodoro breaks distracting, try Flowtime mode. It lets you work as long as you're in "flow" and only suggests a break when your concentration dips.

## 4. Privacy Policy (creapolis.dev/privacy-policy/)
### We value your privacy.
- **Local Data:** Your tasks and focus history are stored on your device.
- **iCloud Sync:** If enabled, data is synced using your personal Apple iCloud account. We never see your data.
- **No Tracking:** We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
- **Health Data:** If you grant permission, we log "Mindful Minutes" to Apple Health. This data never leaves your device and Apple Health.
